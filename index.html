About Me

I'm a recent Master's graduate in Computer Engineering from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, specializing in ASIC/FPGA Design & Verification. I love taking ideas from RTL all the way to real hardware — whether that means writing SystemVerilog testbenches at 2 AM, chasing down timing issues in Vivado, or watching an LED blink exactly the way I intended on an FPGA board.

My journey so far has taken me through RTL coding, simulation, synthesis, timing optimization, and on-board bring-up. Along the way, I've built and verified designs ranging from simple memory blocks to multi-core processors, and tested protocols like SPI, UART, I2C, AXI, and Wishbone.

What excites me most is the challenge of creating reliable, high-performance hardware systems — and the mix of creativity and problem-solving that goes into making them work. While my primary focus is ASIC/SoC and FPGA development, I also enjoy tinkering with Embedded Systems (ARM, MSP430, Raspberry Pi) as a way to connect digital design with the physical world.

Right now, I'm seeking full-time opportunities where I can bring my skills in RTL design, verification, and FPGA prototyping to life and keep pushing the boundaries of digital hardware.
